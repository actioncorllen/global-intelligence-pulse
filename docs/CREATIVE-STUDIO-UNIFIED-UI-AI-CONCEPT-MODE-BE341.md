## New end-to-end functionality (PART A–E)
- **PART A — identity pack now consumed, not just displayed:** `buildConceptGenerationRequest` reads the pack's verified attributes (only `category` today) + the pack's `identityVersion` + `hasAuthorisedReferenceImage`/`rightsEligibility`, and `conceptModeAvailability(pack)` drives mode enablement. Source provenance, confidence, exact SKU/variant identity and the stable identity version are preserved; nothing is invented.
- **PART B — unified Creative Studio** in the real detail: three categories (Product Photos / Lifestyle Visuals / Video Creatives) with one shared product context; three modes (Exact Product / AI Concept / Hybrid Verified); a Studio summary showing selected product + variant, identity + confidence, visual identity version, image permissions, mode, format + aspect ratio (1:1/4:5/16:9; Video fixed 9:16), expected cost, status, and accuracy/clearance.
- **PART C — rights-safe AI Concept pathway (prepared, never executed):** the prompt is built ONLY from the verified `category` + the founder's URL-stripped brief; brand/model/colour/shape/materials/controls/accessories/dimensions are NEVER used; while appearance is unverified (always today) it forces an **original, unbranded** concept and will not name a brand or claim to depict the exact product; every request carries the label **"AI CONCEPT — NOT PRODUCT VERIFIED"**, provenance `ORIGINAL_CONCEPT` with `referenceAsset=null`, `identityValidated=false`, `rightsBasis=ORIGINAL_CONCEPT_TEXT_ONLY`; `executionState=PREPARED_NOT_EXECUTED`, `requiresFounderSpendApproval=true`; `assertConceptRequestSafe` guards every invariant. The UI's "Generate concept — requires founder spend approval" button is **disabled and calls nothing**. No supplier/marketplace/Google/Kalodata pixels or URLs are ever passed; the independently-authorised server-side execution edge/lane is the **prepared next step** (not deployed, no paid call).
- **PART D — visual consistency:** the same identity pack + `identityVersion` drive all three categories (proven stable across category/mode switches by test); where appearance is unverified the Studio reports it truthfully; **no concept can be promoted to a verified commercial asset** (no "use as product image"/promote control exists).
- **PART E — video interface honest:** Video Creatives shows "capability prepared; provider executor not yet connected" + the real-life demonstration shot structure (Problem → Person naturally uses the product → Result → Product close-up, 9:16, ~10–20 s). No generate/export/download control. No slideshow assembly.

**De-duplication decision (transparent):** to avoid two generators in one view, the single gated `CommercialImageGenerator` now lives inside the Studio (the Explorer's `ProductCommerceActions` mount passes `allowCommercialGeneration={false}`). The Studio's Exact/Hybrid gate requires **both** the BE338 readiness gate **and** `supplier_link_allowed` identity — strictly MORE restrictive and more correct (a CONCEPT_ONLY product with an owned image still cannot generate). BE337 Import + BE340 identity panel are unchanged.
